title: Credits for expert chats
category: product_question
---
Chats with astrologers are paid with credits. Credits are charged per minute of an active session and are shown before the session starts.

If a session is interrupted by a technical problem, credits for the unused minutes are returned automatically within 24 hours. If they were not returned, the agent can check the session log and return credits manually.

Purchased credits do not expire. Credits are not converted back to money, except when the Billing team approves a refund under the refund policy.
