---
id: chargeback-legal
title: Chargeback and legal threats
category: billing_subscription
---
If a customer threatens a chargeback, a lawsuit or a complaint to a regulator, do not argue and do not admit fault. Thank the customer, confirm that the case is being reviewed, and escalate it to the Billing lead and the Legal team.

A chargeback opened by the bank takes 30–90 days and blocks the customer's account until it is resolved. Resolving the issue directly with support is usually faster for the customer.

Never threaten the customer with consequences and never share internal policies or legal assessments in the reply.
