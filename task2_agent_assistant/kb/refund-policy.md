id: refund-policy
title: Refund policy for subscriptions
category: refund_request
---
Web subscriptions (paid by card on nebula web checkout) can be refunded within 14 days of the charge if the premium features were not used. Partial refunds for unused months of a yearly plan are not available.

Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are processed by Apple or Google. Support agents cannot refund these purchases directly. The customer must request a refund from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (Order history → Request a refund).

Agents must never promise a refund before the billing team has verified the transaction. Use wording such as "we will review your request" instead of "we will refund you".

Every refund request is reviewed by the Billing team. The standard review time is up to 3 business days. Approved refunds return to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.
