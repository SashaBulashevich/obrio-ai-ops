---
id: expert-misconduct
title: Expert misconduct and off-platform payments
category: expert_complaint
---
Experts are strictly prohibited from asking customers for payment outside the Nebula app (PayPal, bank transfer, crypto, etc.), from requesting personal photos or contact details, and from giving medical, legal or financial instructions.

Customers should never send money to an expert directly. Nebula cannot protect or refund payments made outside the app.

Any report of off-platform payment requests, harassment or harmful advice is escalated to the Trust & Safety team immediately. The expert's account is suspended during the investigation. The customer must not be asked to keep chatting with that expert.
