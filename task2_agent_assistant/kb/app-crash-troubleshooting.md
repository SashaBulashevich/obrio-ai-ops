id: app-crash-troubleshooting
title: App crashes, freezes and white screen
category: technical_issue
---
Basic steps: update Nebula to the latest version, restart the phone, check the internet connection, then reinstall the app. Reinstalling does not delete the account or purchases.

For a bug report, collect the device model, OS version, app version (Profile → Settings → About) and the steps that lead to the crash.

If several customers report the same problem at the same time, it may be an outage. Check the internal status channel and escalate to Tech Support as an incident instead of troubleshooting each customer individually.
