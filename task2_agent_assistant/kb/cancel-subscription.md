---
id: cancel-subscription
title: How to cancel a subscription
category: billing_subscription
---
On iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nebula → Cancel Subscription.

On Android: open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nebula → Cancel.

On web: log in at the Nebula account page → Subscription → Cancel subscription.

Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. After cancellation, premium access stays active until the end of the current paid period, and the customer will not be charged again.

To avoid a renewal charge, the subscription must be cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
