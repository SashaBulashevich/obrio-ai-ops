id: push-notifications
title: Push notifications and daily horoscope reminders
category: technical_issue
---
Ask the customer to check that notifications are allowed for Nebula in the phone settings and that Focus / Do Not Disturb mode is off.

Inside the app, daily horoscope reminders are configured in Profile → Settings → Notifications, where the customer can also choose the delivery time.

Battery saver modes on some Android phones block background notifications; adding Nebula to the battery optimization exceptions usually fixes it.
