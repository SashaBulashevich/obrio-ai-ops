---
id: account-deletion-gdpr
title: Account deletion and personal data requests (GDPR)
category: account_privacy
---
Customers can delete their account in Profile → Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes birth data, readings and chat history within 30 days.

Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; the customer must cancel it separately.

Data deletion or data export requests received by email must be verified: the request must come from the email address linked to the account. Agents must not delete accounts themselves; verified requests are forwarded to the Privacy team, which completes them within 30 days as required by GDPR.
