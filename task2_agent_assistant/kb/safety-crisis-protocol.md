id: safety-crisis-protocol
title: Customers in emotional crisis (internal protocol)
category: safety_crisis
---
If a customer mentions suicidal thoughts, self-harm or not wanting to live, the agent must not send a template or a sales-related reply. The ticket is escalated to the Trust & Safety on-call specialist immediately.

The reply must be written by a human, show care, and encourage the customer to contact local emergency services or a crisis line. International crisis lines are listed at findahelpline.com.

Astrology content must never be presented as a prediction about the customer's life or death. Do not discuss the reading that upset the customer in the first reply.
