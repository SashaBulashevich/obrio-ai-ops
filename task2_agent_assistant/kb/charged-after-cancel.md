title: Charged after cancelling
category: billing_subscription
---
The most common reason is that the subscription was cancelled less than 24 hours before the renewal date, or that only the app was deleted while the subscription stayed active.

Ask the customer for the cancellation confirmation email or a screenshot of the subscription status in the App Store, Google Play or the web account page.

If the cancellation was completed before the renewal date, the charge is a billing error and the Billing team refunds it. For App Store and Google Play purchases, the customer must also contact Apple or Google, because they process the payment.
