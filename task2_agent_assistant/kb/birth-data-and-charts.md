title: Changing birth data, birth chart and rising sign
category: product_question
---
Birth date, time and place can be changed in Profile → Edit birth data. All readings, the birth chart and the rising sign are recalculated automatically after saving.

The rising (ascendant) sign is shown on the Birth Chart screen. It requires an exact birth time; if the time is unknown, the rising sign is not displayed.

Compatibility reports need the partner's birth date. The partner's birth time is optional but makes the report more precise.
