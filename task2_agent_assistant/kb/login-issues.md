id: login-issues
title: Login and verification code problems
category: technical_issue
---
If the verification code email does not arrive, ask the customer to check Spam and Promotions folders and to wait up to 5 minutes. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

Make sure the email address is typed correctly. Customers who signed up with Apple or Google must use "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google" instead of the email code.

If the problem continues, the customer can request a magic login link from the login screen. If the account is still unreachable, escalate to Tech Support with the account email and device model.
