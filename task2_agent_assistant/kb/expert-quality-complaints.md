title: Complaints about expert quality
category: expert_complaint
---
Experts must answer the customer's actual question, stay respectful, and must not end a paid session early without a reason.

When a customer complains about rudeness, generic answers or a session ended early, the Expert Quality team reviews the chat log within 2 business days.

If the complaint is confirmed, the customer receives the credits spent on that session back. Agents should not judge the expert in the reply before the review is finished; thank the customer for the report and explain the review process.
