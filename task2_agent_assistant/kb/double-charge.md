id: double-charge
title: Double or unexpected charges
category: billing_subscription
---
A second charge is often a temporary authorization hold from the bank, not a real payment. Pending authorizations disappear automatically within 3–5 business days.

If two completed charges are visible, ask the customer for the last 4 digits of the card, the date and the amount of each charge, or a screenshot of the bank statement. Never ask for the full card number or CVV.

The Billing team verifies duplicates in the payment system. Confirmed duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

If the customer says they never authorized any subscription, treat the case as a possible unauthorized charge and escalate it to Billing with high priority.
