---
id: premium-not-activated
title: Paid but premium is not activated
category: billing_subscription
---
Ask the customer to tap "Restore purchases" in Profile → Settings. This re-checks the purchase with the App Store or Google Play and usually activates premium immediately.

Make sure the customer is logged into the same Nebula account and the same Apple ID or Google account that was used for the purchase.

If premium is still not active after restoring, collect the order number from the purchase receipt email and escalate to Billing. The Billing team activates premium manually after verifying the payment.
